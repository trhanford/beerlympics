Game Rules

Tap each game to expand the rules.

Need more detail? Ask The Ref — they know every rule, every edge case, and all the house rules too.
Beer Pong
  • 10 cups per team in a triangle. Alternate turns, one shot at a time.
  • Made shot = cup removed and consumed. Both teammates make = balls back.
  • When all cups are gone, losing team gets one redemption turn.
Flip Cup
  • Teams line up on opposite sides. Drink, set cup on edge, then flip it over.
  • Next teammate only starts after the previous flip lands cleanly.
  • First team fully done wins. No mid-flip hand switching.
Beerio Kart
  • Each player starts a Mario Kart race with one beer. Finish it before the finish line.
  • No drinking while the kart is moving — pull over first.
  • Cross the line before finishing = that score doesn't count.
Die
  • Toss the die so it bounces cleanly on the opposing team's half.
  • One-handed catch negates the point for offense.
  • Missed catch = 1 pt; die hits floor untouched = 2 pts.
  • Play to the host's target score (commonly 9 or 11).
Drinkball
  • One cup per player, one ball in play. Shoot to land in an opponent's cup.
  • Made shot = that player drinks and the cup resets.
  • No goaltending, blocking with your cup hand, or body contact.
Bag Toss
  • Boards 27 ft apart; partners play from opposite ends.
  • Bag in hole = 3 pts. Bag on board = 1 pt. Cancellation scoring each round.
  • First to exactly 21 wins. Going over busts you back.
  • All throws must be underhand and from behind the foul line.
Darts
  • 301 format, double out. Count down from 301 to exactly zero.
  • Final dart must hit a double or bullseye to close it out.
  • Going below zero is a bust — revert to score before that turn.
  • Teams alternate turns; partners alternate who throws each round.
Rage Cage
  • All 4 players around a table filled with cups.
  • Bounce a ping pong ball into your cup, then pass it clockwise.
  • Make it before the person ahead of you = stack your cup on theirs.
  • They must draw a new cup from the pile and start over.
  • Team that eliminates both opponents first wins.
Kan Jam
  • Dinger (1 pt): deflected frisbee touches the side of the kan.
  • Deuce (2 pts): thrown frisbee hits kan directly, no partner help.
  • Bucket (3 pts): partner deflects frisbee into the kan's opening.
  • Instant win: thrower puts it through the slot unassisted.
  • Play to exactly 21. Go over and you drop back to 11.
Spikeball
  • Serve off the net; each team gets up to 3 touches to return.
  • No designated sides — move freely around the net after the serve.
  • Point when opponents can't return, or ball hits the rim or bounces twice.
  • First to 21, win by 2. No spin serves allowed.
Quarters
  • Players sit in a circle with their drink in front of them.
  • Someone spins a quarter and calls a flicker while it's still spinning.
  • The flicker tries to knock the quarter into another player's drink.
  • Hit = that player chugs for the full duration of the next spin.
  • Up to 2 others may tap the quarter to keep it spinning longer.
  • Guard your drink with 2 fingers from one hand only — no arms or cupping.
  • When the spin stops, the drinker gets to spin next and call a new flicker.
  • Miss = quarter passes to the called flicker, who now spins and calls.
