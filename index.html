Game Rules

Tap each game to expand the rules.

Beer Pong
  • Set up 10 cups in a triangle for each team.
  • Teams alternate tosses; one player shoots at a time.
  • A made shot removes one cup and that cup must be consumed.
  • Both teammates make in one turn = balls back for another turn.
  • When all cups are gone, the losing team gets one redemption turn.
Flip Cup
  • Teams line up shoulder-to-shoulder on opposite sides of the table.
  • First player drinks, sets cup on edge, and flips until it lands upside down.
  • Next teammate starts only after the previous flip is completed.
  • First team to have every player complete their flip wins the round.
  • No hand-switching mid-flip and no touching a teammate’s cup.
Beerio Kart
  • Each player starts a Mario Kart race with one unopened beer.
  • You must finish your drink before crossing the finish line.
  • No drinking while your kart is moving; pull over to drink.
  • If you cross before finishing, that race score does not count.
  • Fastest valid combined team finish wins.
Die
  • Two teams face each other with drinks at table corners.
  • Players toss a die aiming for a clean hit on the table and high bounce.
  • Catches with one hand negate points; missed catches score for offense.
  • Standard scoring: 1 point for a miss, 2 if the die hits floor untouched.
  • Play to the target score set by the host (commonly 9 or 11).
Drinkball
  • Place one cup in front of each player and one ball in play.
  • Teams pass and shoot trying to land the ball in an opponent cup.
  • Made shot = opposing player drinks and that cup resets.
  • No goaltending, blocking with cup hand, or body contact.
  • First team to the agreed score wins.
